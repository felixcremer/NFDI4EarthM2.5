# Pilot support


This document captures the support Measure 2.5 Advancing Tools offered for the pilots of the NFDI4Earth project and the interaction with the Academy.



## Consulting

We participated in the first pilot meeting to explain the concept of a data cube and to investigate which data handling solutions are used in the pilot studies. 
There were 14 pilot studies in total in the first round of which five used data cube technologies or were interested in working with data cubes. 
These pilots used data cubes for model data and satellite observation data.
We met with three interested pilots to further discuss the use of data cubes in general and the use cases of data cubes and their advantages and disadvantages. 


## Development support

Fruitful interactions have taken place with the NFDI4Earth pilot "Statistical Learning to assess factors underlying environmental changes". 
They transfered the application of new statistical methods to a data cube framework and approached us with several
support requests. We could solve most of the technical questions the pilot posed to us and contributed to its success
in creating their its product. The following list of github issues was solved in relation to the support of this pilot:

# Interaction with the Academy


## DataXPlorers Hackathon

To dissiminate the usage of data cube technologies to early career scientists we provided the a data cube use case to the [DataXplorers hackathon](https://www.nfdi.de/dataxplorers-hackathon/). 
In the hackathon the participants computed bio climatologies to learn how to work efficiently with large gridded datasets in the cloud.
This enabled the Academy fellows to work with data cube in a confined environment and to get hands-on experience in the handling of data cubes. 
The main focus of this hackathon challenge was to show the usage of data cubes for the analysis of large raster data. 
Hereby we showed how data can be prepared as a data cube.
This preparation of the data can enhance the reusability since the analysis via data cube tools can be easier automated and applied on new datasets.
One of the participants of the hackathon published their resulting scripts and results here: 
https://github.com/tfohrmann/dataxplorers-challenge-5

## DWD COSMO-REA Datacube usage in NFDI4Earth Academy Hackathon

The DWD COSMO-REA data cube has been used in the NFDI4Earth Academy hackathon.
We converted the provided NetCDF dataset of the COSMO-REA data cube which has been prepared by DKRZ into the zarr format.
We provided the data in spatial and temporal chunking to enable time series analysis as well as mapping capabilities.
The data cube optimized for time series access has a chunking of 20x20 spatially and 1000 time steps in time. 
The data cube optimized for spatial access has a chunking of a full spatial extent separated in single time steps.

## Introduction to DataCube analysis with Julia

In the scope of an Academy Online-Workshop we provided an introduction to using data cubes in Julia.
Through a combination of theoretical knowledge and practical exercises, participants were enabled to create, analyze and use DataCubes for various applications in the earth system sciences and beyond.
